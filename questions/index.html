Questions below are gameplay, retention and design only. Most carry our recommendation and a default, so nothing waits on a reply. Four are marked in red — those change what we build. The fastest useful reply is the number and one line.

Gameplay Questions 1–8

1

When does the new puzzle appear?

Midnight, or with the printed paper around 05:00?

We recommend Midnight. Nobody awake at 02:00 should be locked out of today.

2

Old puzzles

Should solving one late count toward the streak?

We recommend No. It earns points, not streak days — otherwise a reader clears a month of archive on Sunday and claims thirty days they never played.

3

Hints

Readers earn points by solving, and spend them on one hint: a letter in শব্দভেদ, a digit in সুডোকু, a word in শব্দ সন্ধান.

We recommend Hints stay the only thing points buy. No shop.

4

The Bangla keyboard

শব্দভেদ ships its own on-screen type case — varnamala in বর্গ order, kars and hasant on separate rows. Built for a reader who has never typed Bangla on a phone. Does that suit your readers, or would you expect Avro phonetic typing?

If we do not hear back Ours ships.

5

A bigger-key version of that keyboard?

It carries 61 keys, so on a phone each one lands around 30px tall — fine for most people, tight for anyone with weaker eyesight or less steady hands. A large-key mode would show fewer keys at a time and make each roughly half again as big.

We recommend Deciding now rather than later. Once forty games share this keyboard, changing it means re-testing all forty.

6

সুডোকু

১–৯ by default with a 1–9 switch, difficulty rising Monday to Sunday. Does that match your print page?

7

Daily counts

শব্দ সন্ধান 12 words · শব্দভেদ 8×8, print parity · শব্দফুল 24 words. Set any differently?

8

শব্দফুল’s “report a word” button

It needs an inbox or a form to send reports to. Otherwise we remove it.

Retention Questions 9–13

9

Streak freeze

Miss a day, watch one video, keep the streak.

We recommend It cannot be bought — not with points, not with money. Charging a reader to undo a loss the game created is a trick, and we would rather not build it.

10

Leaderboard

Daily, per game, top 100 plus your own rank. No all-time table.

Why A daily reset means a new reader is never staring at an unreachable score.

11

Sharing

Today the hub shares plain text, which shows off a score but brings nobody in. We want a Prothom Alo image card: solve time, a spoiler-free picture of the grid, and a link that opens the same puzzle — so whoever receives it plays exactly what you played. WhatsApp, Messenger, Facebook, Instagram.

We need Your agreement that readers may share a card carrying the masthead, plus the wordmark from 14.

12

Reminders

One notification only — streak at risk, evening of a missed day. Do you want it?

We recommend Yes. It is the one message in this product readers thank you for.

13

What else brings readers back?

App placement, home-page promotion, email — tell us which are in scope and we will design for them.

Visual Questions 14–17

14

Brand kit

We assumed #EE4023, read off your portal. Please send the official red, the wordmark and the lockup. The masthead is holding an empty space, and the share card in 11 cannot ship without it.

If we do not hear back The hub launches without your wordmark.

15

One palette across every game

Newsprint paper, your red, and a cyan measured off your own print scans — used only for blocked cells.

We recommend Keeping it one system. Per-game colours would give us forty palettes to police.

16

Dark mode?

Worth your call rather than only ours — night reading on a phone is real.

We recommend Light only for now. Print parity is the whole idea. The colours are already variables, so it stays easy to add later.

17

The look

Four games and the hub are playable end to end. Anything you would change about the layout, the type, the density, the way a puzzle reads on a phone? This is the cheapest moment to say so.

Ads and layout Questions 18–19

18

Which ad formats, and where?

Rewarded video is already covered in 9. For everything else we need the units you sell and where they may appear.

We recommend:

  • Hub catalogue — a native unit between game rows, in the feed rather than over it.
  • Game front page — a banner above the fold, before play starts.
  • After a solve — a banner on the result card. The best slot in the product: the reader has finished, nothing is interrupted, attention is high.
  • Nothing during play, and no full-screen ad before a daily puzzle. A reader who meets a full-screen ad on the way to today’s crossword is a reader who stops coming daily. This is the one placement we would argue against.
19

The anchored banner is the one that costs us layout

A game page is a fixed-height screen: board, keyboard, nothing scrolls. The board takes whatever the rest leaves — currently the viewport height minus about 17rem of chrome and keyboard. A sticky banner pinned to the bottom comes straight out of the board, and on a small phone that shrinks every cell and the letters inside them. It also lands exactly where the keyboard already sits.

We recommend No anchored banner on a game screen. Put it on the hub and the result card, where the page can simply grow. If you need one on the game screen, tell us early — the board sizing is what changes.

Editorial Questions 20–23

20

Your ban list

We screen the dictionary against 27 blocked word-roots we chose ourselves. Please send Prothom Alo’s own.

If we do not hear back Our list stands — a Nagorik judgement sitting on your masthead.

21

Words no human has read — here they are

শব্দফুল accepts 108,900 distinct Bangla words across the 30-day pack. They are machine-screened against the blocked roots in 20, but nobody has read them. Asking you to approve a list we never showed you would be meaningless, so here is all of it, one word per line: word-list.txt

Would you like a sampled human review before launch?

If we do not hear back We take the screen as accepted.

22

Your editorial guidance

Puzzle themes, crossword clues and quiz questions go out under your masthead, so they should follow your rules rather than ours. Please send whatever editorial guidance applies — and name the editor who signs off.

If we do not hear back We keep to everyday subjects and do not send borderline items to you.

23

Who writes the puzzles after we hand over?

We author the first packs. At some point your side takes it on — we need the team or person who will own it, and roughly when they start, so we can plan the training. Puzzles are added through a simple editor; nobody needs to be technical.

Also worth settling: should the flagship carry শব্দভেদ or শব্দজট?

If we do not hear back We rename the two catalogue games and leave the heroes alone.

Rights Questions 26–28

26

The printed archive

We transcribed five of your printed শব্দজট puzzles into শব্দভেদ, and they are on the preview. We need the digital-reuse position confirmed in writing.

This one carries a schedule cost, which is why it is marked. Your archive is decades of puzzles already built and already edited. Without it, every puzzle has to be designed from nothing — a crossword grid that fills cleanly in Bangla, clues written and checked, the whole thing tested. That work is slow, it does not speed up with more people on it, and it is the largest single risk to the launch date. Reusing the archive removes that risk almost entirely.

If we do not hear back We go on scanning and digitising. Tell us to stop and we stop.

27

A GPL word list on your surface

শব্দফুল promises never to refuse a real Bangla word. Keeping that promise meant shipping the 108,900-word list in 21, built from the open Hunspell bn_BD dictionary, which carries a GPL notice. That is your call to make — and we would rather you made it than found it.

If we do not hear back It ships as built, with the notice.

Are you pursuing a licence? It would replace the list above and remove 27 entirely.

If we do not hear back We assume not.

If you answer only a few: 14, 20, 26 and 27. Those four change what we build. Everything else has a default and will proceed on it.
